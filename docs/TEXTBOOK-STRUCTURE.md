# Ross E-Drive University — DPS Checklists

## Permit Checklist (Teen/Adult)

- Completed applicable program
- Ross E-Drive certificate
- TDLR packet (if PTDE)
- Identity documents
- Residency documents

## License Checklist (Teen)

- Valid learner permit
- 44 hours behind-the-wheel
- ITTD completion
- License certificate
- Parent/guardian signature

## License Checklist (Adult)

- Completed Adult ADE
- Ross E-Drive certificate
- DPS written and driving test scheduled
